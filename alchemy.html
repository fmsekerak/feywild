Alchemy

Alchemy uses Alchemist’s Tools or a Poisoner’s Kit, with Wisdom or Intelligence as shown below. Alchemy is one of the branches that requires consecutive crafting checks. A failed check grants no progress; three consecutive failures end the project and consume its materials.

Refer to the Crafting Guide for full rules and recipe details.
