Feywild Bestiary

A lightweight player-facing index for the creatures that matter most to this campaign.

Fey Dragons

Fey Dragons are treated as characters rather than simple mounts. Their personalities, values, and tests of trust reflect the season they embody, and a bond is something earned over time.

🌱

Spring Dragon

Playful and curious, associated with renewal, hope, growth, healing, and illusion.

🔥

Summer Dragon

Proud and reckless, associated with passion, courage, protection, and destructive fire.

🍂

Autumn Dragon

Wistful and wise, associated with change, memory, melancholy, divination, and hidden paths.

🧊

Winter Dragon

Cold and calculating, associated with endurance, death, secrets, ice, and dream-walking.

🌘

Twilight Dragon

Mysterious and silent, tied to the boundary between courts and the difficult work of moving between opposed realms without losing yourself.

🌺

Floral Dragons

The campaign also draws creature inspiration from The Field Guide to Floral Dragons. Keep detailed stat blocks in your private DM resources and use this page as the public index.
