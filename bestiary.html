Fey Dragons & Bestiary

Fey Dragons are more than mounts. They are characters, guardians, rivals, and keepers of old truths. Their bonds are earned through trials rather than simply claimed.

🌸 Spring Dragon

Playful and curious. Its magic leans toward renewal, hope, healing, and illusion.

☀️ Summer Dragon

Proud and reckless. Its magic reflects passion, courage, fire, and protection.

🍂 Autumn Dragon

Wistful and wise. Its magic favors change, memory, divination, and hidden knowledge.

❄️ Winter Dragon

Cold and calculating. Its magic favors endurance, dreams, secrets, ice, and death.

The Bonding Ritual

A dragon chooses its partner. A bond might require a riddle, an offered memory, a dangerous service, or a trial tied to the spirit of its season. Early bonds may begin with a tiny familiar-like form and deepen as the party completes major trials.

As the bond grows, the dragon can become more powerful, communicate more directly, reveal ancient memories, and eventually take on a full rider-bearing form.
