Adventure Trail

Campaign Overview

A spoiler-light path through the major chapters of the campaign.

🎪

Witchlight Carnival

The campaign begins at a wondrous carnival that hides a crossing into the Feywild.

🐊

Murkendraw

The party enters a vast, foggy swamp filled with shifting paths, strange factions, and the first serious dangers of the Feywild.

👑

The Seasonal Courts

Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter each have their own politics, traditions, problems, and reasons to distrust the others.

🐉

Fey Dragons

Ancient dragons become increasingly important as the party earns trust, survives trials, and learns more about the balance of the realm.

✨

Endgame

The deeper secrets of the Feywild and its fractured powers converge. Keep the specifics in your DM notes until the table reaches them.
