Campaign Trail

A compact overview of the campaign path. Keep this page player-facing or expand it into a DM-only section later.

🎪 Witchlight Carnival · Level 1 → 2

The carnival introduces the party to the strange rules of the Feywild and provides passage through a fey crossing.

🐊 Murkendraw · Level 2 → 4

The party enters the swamp, faces harengon raiders, bullywug politics, unsettling travel encounters, and Bavlorna Blightstraw.

👑 Seasonal Courts

Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter each hold pieces of a larger mystery and have their own politics, trials, and grudges.

🐉 Fey Dragons

The dragons guard forgotten truths and require personal trials before they will trust the party or surrender what they protect.

✨ Reforging & Sacrifice

The later campaign centers on restoring what was divided, with the final ritual demanding a meaningful personal cost.
