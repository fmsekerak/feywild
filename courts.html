Seasonal Courts

Each court shapes its land, culture, politics, and magic around the nature of its season. They share a fractured history, but their values pull them in very different directions.

🌸

Spring Court

Rebirth, growth, passion, temptation, and a realm in constant motion. Beauty and excess sit dangerously close together.

☀️

Summer Court

Strength, courage, revelry, ceremony, and blazing emotion. Its glory can become pride, indulgence, and fury.

🍂

Autumn Court

Change, tradition, preparation, twilight, and hard-earned power. Autumn respects those who can survive transition.

❄️

Winter Court

Patience, survival, secrets, endings, and ruthless control. Winter watches longer than others expect.

🌿

Wild Fey

Untamed tribes, spirits, shapeshifters, and wanderers who rejected the rigid structures of the Seasonal Courts.

The Court Problem

With the great Fey Queens absent, the courts have grown more independent, suspicious, and competitive. Their seasonal magic has become increasingly unstable, and each court has its own reason to resist giving up power.
