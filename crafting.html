Core Rule

Crafting is done in 2-hour intervals, with one check per interval. A success grants 2 hours of progress; once progress equals the required crafting time, the item is complete. A failure grants no progress. Some crafts, such as Alchemy, require consecutive checks. Three consecutive failures result in total failure and loss of materials.

During a long rest, you may spend up to 2 hours crafting if you do nothing else. While doing so, you have disadvantage on Perception checks and a −5 penalty to passive Perception.

Crafting Rolls

Each profession lists its related ability modifier and tool. Most branches also need an appropriate work area or heat source depending on the craft.

Expertise & Help

Tool expertise is included in the crafting roll modifier. Other boosts generally do not apply; the main alternative is sustained help from another skilled crafter.

Taking 10

Double the crafting interval from 2 hours to 4 hours to take a 10 on the crafting roll. If a proficient helper assists for the full duration, the check can be completed in the normal 2 hours.

Workdays

A full crafting workday produces 8 hours of progress. Characters generally cannot exceed 8 hours of crafting work in a day without risking Exhaustion.
