World Guide

The Feywild

A living plane where feeling can bend the landscape and neither time nor distance behaves quite as expected.

A World That Feels Back

The Feywild responds to emotion. Joy, anger, malice, curiosity, and grief can echo through flowers, animals, weather, and the land itself. Even ordinary scenery can seem alert to the mood of those passing through it.

Geography is mutable as well. Roads may change, travel times may stretch or collapse, and a route that was easy yesterday may become a long journey tomorrow.

🤝

Honor the Bargain

Promises and oaths matter. A careless agreement can carry more weight here than a signed contract elsewhere.

⚖️

Equal Exchange

Taking without giving invites trouble. Gifts, favors, and debts are part of the social fabric of the courts.

🗣️

Truth Has Teeth

Fey etiquette prizes careful wording. Saying exactly what you mean can be safer than saying more than you intend.
