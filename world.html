The Feywild

The Feywild responds to emotion, bends distance and time, and treats promises, names, gifts, and hospitality as forces with real magical weight.

Unfettered Emotion Mutable Roads True Names Fey Bargains Cold Iron

The land itself may react to those traveling through it: flowers tremble during arguments, grass withers beneath malice, and roads can change from one journey to the next.

Three Rules of the Fey Courts

  1. Speak no falsehoods.
  2. Always honor oaths and promises.
  3. Never take anything without giving something of equal value.

Travel, Names, and Bargains

Time & Distance

A route that takes an hour one day might take days the next. Returning home can also mean returning to a very different point in time.

True Names

A true name defines a being deeply enough to attract its attention and can be used in powerful rituals and magic.

Hospitality

Hosts and guests are bound by courtesy and reciprocity, although what counts as rude can differ from one fey to another.

Gifts & Contracts

A gift may create an expectation of equal return. Careless exchanges can become accidental fey bargains.
